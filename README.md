# Check My Boat

A guided yacht acceptance assistant for charter skippers. The app walks you through a full boat check-in — zone by zone, in the most efficient order — so you don't miss anything important before casting off.

Available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does the app actually do?**
Check My Boat guides you through a structured zone-by-zone inspection of a charter yacht — covering interior, engine, cockpit, exterior, rig, and safety equipment. It asks one question at a time, lets you log defects with photos, and generates a clean acceptance report at the end.

**Does the app replace the official charter company check-in?**
No. Check My Boat is a personal tool to help you not miss anything. You should always complete the official check-in with the charter manager and sign their documents as required. Use the app alongside the official process, not instead of it.

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes, fully. No internet connection is required at any point. Everything runs on your device.

**What boat types does the app support?**
Monohulls and catamarans. Select your boat type at the start of each check-in and the checklist adjusts accordingly.

**What check-in modes are available?**
Three modes: Express (critical items only, ~15 min), Standard (critical + important, ~40 min, recommended), and Full (everything, ~60 min). You can switch mode at any time during the check-in.

**Can I log a defect with a photo?**
Yes. On any check item, tap the Defect button to take or select a photo. The app automatically adds the zone name, date, and time to the photo caption. Photos are saved to your device's photo library.

**How do I generate the report?**
Tap the Stages list icon during the check-in, then tap "Finish check-in" at the bottom. The report is generated immediately and can be shared via any app on your device (email, messaging, AirDrop, etc.).

**The report is always in English — can I change that?**
The acceptance report is generated in English regardless of interface language. This is intentional — charter companies and marina staff across the Mediterranean work in English, and a consistent English report is more universally useful than a translated one.

**Can I change the interface language?**
Yes. The interface is available in English, Russian, and Spanish. Select your language on first launch or change it at any time in the app settings.

**Will my check-in be lost if I close the app?**
No. Progress is saved after every answer. You can close the app, restart your phone, and resume exactly where you left off.

**Can I start a new check-in without finishing the previous one?**
Yes. Open the app, tap "New check-in" and confirm. The previous session will be discarded.

**What happens if I tap "Not OK" on an item?**
The item is flagged in the report under "Flagged Items". The check-in continues — the app does not block progress on flagged items. At the end you will see a summary of everything flagged.

**What is the "Talk with the charter company" section?**
This is a list of standard questions worth asking the charter manager before you leave — anchor chain length, engine RPM, tank capacities, echo sounder calibration, and others. You can mark each question as Asked or Not relevant, and add your own custom questions.

**Does the app require an account or login?**
No. There are no accounts, no registration, and no login. Open the app and start immediately.

## Content Notes

The check-in checklist is based on standard Mediterranean and Turkish charter practice. Equipment lists may vary by region, charter company, and vessel. If an item on the checklist is not present on your boat, skip it — the app is designed to be used flexibly, not rigidly.

If you find an error or omission in the checklist content, please report it via email. We treat content accuracy as a priority.
