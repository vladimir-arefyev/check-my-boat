# Privacy Policy — Check My Boat

**Effective date:** June 2026
**Last updated:** June 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Check My Boat ("the App"), a guided yacht acceptance assistant for charter skippers. Check My Boat is developed and operated by Vladimir Arefev ("I", "me", or "my").

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and your rights in relation to it. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

I collect the information you voluntarily enter during use of the App:

- **Boat type** (monohull or catamaran) — used to configure the checklist for your vessel
- **Boat name** (optional) — displayed on the acceptance report if entered
- **Check-in mode** (Express, Standard, or Full) — used to filter checklist items
- **Interface language** (English, Russian, or Spanish) — used to display the app in your chosen language
- **Check-in answers** (OK, Not OK, Missing, Skipped) — recorded locally to generate your report
- **Defect descriptions** — text notes you add when logging a defect
- **Custom questions** — questions you add to the charter manager Q&A list

### 2.2 Photos

When you log a defect, the App requests access to your camera or photo library to attach a photo. Photos are saved **to your device's photo library only**. They are not uploaded to any server or transmitted anywhere.

### 2.3 Information Collected Automatically

**I do not collect any personal data automatically.** The App does not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or any third-party tracking tools. The App makes no network requests during normal use.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

All data entered in the App is used solely to:

- Guide you through the check-in flow
- Generate your acceptance report at the end of the check-in
- Save your progress so you can resume if the app is closed
- Display your interface in your chosen language

I do not use your data for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose beyond the core function of the App.

---

## 4. Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device only**, using iOS on-device storage. This includes your check-in answers, defect notes, custom questions, language preference, and boat details.

No data is transmitted to any server. I have no access to any information you enter in the App.

Progress is saved after every answer to prevent loss if the App is closed unexpectedly.

---

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

**I do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third party.**

The App operates entirely offline. There are no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social integrations embedded in the App.

The acceptance report you generate is shared only if you choose to share it yourself — using the Share button within the App, which triggers iOS's standard share sheet. I have no access to what you share or where.

I may disclose information only if required to do so by applicable law or a valid legal process.

---

## 6. Camera and Photo Library Access

The App requests camera access and photo library write access when you attempt to log a defect photo. These permissions are used solely to capture and save defect photos during the check-in. You can deny these permissions — the rest of the App will continue to function normally without photo logging.

Photos saved to your device's photo library remain under your full control and are governed by your device's standard privacy settings.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and provided personal information, please contact me and I will take steps to address it.

---

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can:

- **Clear a check-in** — start a new check-in to discard the current session
- **Delete all data** — uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data from your device
- **Change your language** — update the interface language at any time in app settings
- **Revoke camera access** — go to iOS Settings → Check My Boat → Camera and toggle off

There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request, as I hold no personal data about you.
